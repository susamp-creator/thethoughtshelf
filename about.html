THE WRITER

Hello, I’m Sneha Susan Thomas.

I write The Thought Shelf, a small and slowly growing collection of thoughts on life, people, society, and the things we often overlook. It began as a place to put down the thoughts that stayed with me a little longer than expected.

Some are about things we notice every day; others are about the things we rarely stop to question. This is simply my way of putting them somewhere — and perhaps leaving a little room on your shelf for them too.
